# Contributing to OpenChat

Thanks for your interest in OpenChat!

## Pull requests are by invitation

To keep the project maintainable, **pull requests are only accepted from
approved contributors**. PRs from other accounts are closed automatically.

If you'd like to contribute:

1. **Open an issue first** describing the bug or the change you have in mind.
2. If it's a good fit for the project, we'll discuss the approach there and
   invite you to contribute.

## Bug reports

Bug reports are always welcome — please include your OS, the OpenChat version,
and steps to reproduce.
